Defensibility

1. Widget distribution moat

"Powered by CVPass" on 1,000 job boards = brand lock-in. Each embed is a permanent distribution channel with zero CAC.

2. Multi-language complexity

EU jobs mix Finnish+English, German+English in one JD. US competitors can't do this. Deep prompt engineering per language pair.

3. Integration switching cost

Once an ATS integrates our API, switching = rewriting their screening pipeline. Same moat as Stripe. Technically possible, practically never happens.

4. Model-agnostic

Today Claude, tomorrow GPT or Llama. Value is in the API layer (auth, billing, widget, analytics) -- not the LLM. AI is a commodity; distribution isn't.
