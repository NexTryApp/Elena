02 Problem

Selling clothes in Finland is broken

  • 1
    Tori.fi is a classifieds board, not a marketplace.
    No payments, no shipping, no buyer protection. Like Craigslist in 2024. Median listing gets 0 messages.
  • 2
    Vinted entered Finland only in Nov 2023 — still early.
    €10B+ GMV globally but Finnish market barely started. Bought (ex-Zadaa) just raised $1.5M — validating the vertical.
  • 3
    Pricing is a guessing game.
    Sellers don't know what things are worth. 40% of listings are priced wrong and never sell. No AI, no market data.
  • 4
    Businesses have no platform.
    Outlet stores, returns, open-box — Finnish retailers have no dedicated resale channel for B2C surplus inventory.
Sources: Tori.fi user research (2025), Vinted market coverage map, internal seller surveys
03 Solution

Full-stack resale marketplace
built for Nordic trust culture

For Sellers

  • AI
    AI auto-fill — snap a photo, get title + description + price suggestion in 3 seconds (Qwen-VL)
  • $
    Dutch auctions — price drops automatically until someone bites. Zero negotiation friction.
  • B
    Business batch upload — outlets, second-hand stores, returns. Upload 100 items at once with size variants.
  • 💰
    Instant payouts — Stripe Connect. Money in your bank next day.

For Buyers

  • 🛡
    Buyer protection — payment held in escrow until buyer confirms receipt. Disputes with evidence.
  • 👗
    Virtual try-on — see how clothes look on you before buying (ComfyUI + SAM3)
  • 📦
    Integrated shipping — Posti SmartPost labels generated automatically. Or meetup zones.
  • 💬
    Real-time chat — negotiate, send offers, counter-offer — all in-app with WebSocket.

07 Business Model

Three revenue streams from day one

5% + €0.70

Buyer Protection Fee

Charged to buyer on every transaction. Min €1.00. Covers escrow, dispute resolution, fraud prevention.

On €25 avg item: €1.95 fee (7.8% effective take rate)

€0.40

Shipping Margin

Fixed margin on every shipped order. Posti base €3.90 → buyer pays €4.30. We keep €0.40.

~70% of orders are shipped = €0.28 blended per txn

€0.99

Listing Bumps

Push listing to top of feed for 24h. Optional, seller-paid. ~5% attach rate expected.

Future: business subscriptions, promoted listings, featured brands.

Blended take rate at scale
~10%
Comparable to Vinted (10.5%), Depop (10%), Poshmark (20%). Conservative — room to add business subscriptions and ads.
